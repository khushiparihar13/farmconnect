FarmConnect FAQ

What is FarmConnect?
FarmConnect is an online platform that connects farmers with job seekers looking for work in the agriculture sector.
How does FarmConnect work?
Farmers can post job listings, and job seekers can browse and apply for jobs, making recruitment easier.
Is FarmConnect free to use?
FarmConnect offers both free and premium plans for job postings.
How do I post a job on FarmConnect?
You can create an account, fill in job details, and publish your listing.
How do I apply for farm jobs?
Browse job listings, select a job, and click “Apply.” You may need to create a profile.
